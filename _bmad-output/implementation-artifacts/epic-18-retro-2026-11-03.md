**Date:** 2026-11-03

## Epic Summary

All Epic 18 stories delivered at 100% completion. Validating admission moved from per-type Go webhooks to Kubernetes ValidatingAdmissionPolicies under `config/vap/`, organized in three tiers (universal shared, credential-source shared, CRD-specific). The migration fixed 35 pre-existing immutability gaps (4 `spec.path` + 31 `spec.name`), deleted the bulk of validation-only webhook files, retained mutating defaulters plus RabbitMQ cross-resource validation and 16 not-yet-migrated Go validators, raised CSV `minKubeVersion` to 1.30.0 / OCP 4.17, and shipped `docs/admission-control.md` plus agent-facing `project-context.md` updates.

This retrospective closes tracking only — no leftover code patches were executed in-session. Follow-ups are tracked as sprint-status `action_items`.

## What Went Well

1. **100% delivery:** 18.1–18.6 (including 18.1.1) done. Shared-policy strategy avoided ~one-VAP-per-CRD sprawl.
2. **Shared Tier 1 VAPs paid off:** One CEL expression per universal rule covers dozens of CRDs; the 35 gap CRDs were fixed by resource-list membership, not per-file Go edits.
3. **Parameterized target-namespace guard worked:** `vco-vap-params` ConfigMap + CEL `params` replicated `ValidateTargetNamespaceUpdate` without keeping Go code.
4. **envtest + Kind coverage landed:** Story 18.4 delivered comprehensive envtest specs and Kind integration smoke for VAP enforcement; leftover Kind VAP pollution was identified and cleaned up in-story.
5. **Documentation gate met:** `docs/admission-control.md` covers three-tier architecture, bindings/params, residual webhooks, and version requirements. Contributing guide and project-context now describe VAP-first new-type workflow.
6. **Residual inventory stayed honest:** Story 18.5 corrected UserpassAuthEngineUser story-doc drift (webhook removed in 18.3) against live `*_webhook.go` files.

## What Could Be Improved

1. **Story specs drifted from shipped inventory.** Early stories listed UserpassAuthEngineUser as a residual mutator and used approximate Tier 2/3 counts; final docs had to re-verify against `config/vap/` and remaining webhooks.
2. **Published bundle lagged bases.** Bases/config have `minKubeVersion: 1.30.0` and `com.redhat.openshift.versions: v4.17`, but the published bundle still shows CSV `1.29.0` and annotations `v4.16` until regeneration — easy to miss without an explicit regenerate step in the story.
3. **16 Go validators remain.** Migration prioritized shared + high-value CRD-specific CEL; leftover type-specific validators are intentional debt, not accidental omissions — but they blur the "VAP-first" story if not called out.
4. **CEL `omitempty` / absent-field pitfalls.** Several Tier 3 policies needed `has()` guards after envtest failures; complexity grows quickly for conditional field rules.
